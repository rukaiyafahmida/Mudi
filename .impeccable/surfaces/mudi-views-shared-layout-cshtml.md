# Mudi shared interface

Mode: Operate. Preserve all routes, content, images, prices, roles and form bindings. Build directly in code. Chosen by the user: Catalogue shelves.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A grocery catalogue of browsable shelves, with management as a clear working ledger.

OWN-WORLD: Ink and pale text at night; warm paper and deep amber by day. Condensed display lettering, neutral forms, fixed-ratio artwork.

STORY: Browse categories, inspect price and stock, collect a basket, order; administrators maintain the same catalogue.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim left task rail, spacious catalogue heading and search, category navigation, image shelf with visible product names and prices. Phones use a compact top bar and drawer. Primary action stays beside its product.

FORM: User-selected streaming title-card wall challenger, pop-culture-shelf-streaming-title-card-wall; seed 21994b0d. Signature: focused shelf lifts its product border and reveals supporting copy; horizontal scroll snaps with keyboard controls. Reduced motion preserves state without animation.
